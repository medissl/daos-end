# Milestone-two image prompt set

Built-in imagegen mode throughout; no API/CLI fallback or third-party artist
style was requested. Selected source identifiers and reuse sizes are in
`assets/icons/manifest.json` and `assets/ART_SOURCES.md`.

## Focal character and environment directions

Character generation: original full-body transparent production sprite,
restrained Chinese line-and-wash, asymmetric ivory shoulder cape over mineral
jade cross-collar robe, practical cloth herb pouch, small hair knot with wood
pin, cross-legged front pose, both palms resting on knees with coherent fingers,
covered feet, low oval stone slab. Deliberate tapered contours, two or three
robe value masses, quiet adult face. No scenery, text, armor, jewelry, halo,
hair loops, glossy shading, all-over grain or synthetic speckling.

Targeted identity-preserving edit: preserve face, pose, silhouette, cape,
collar, pouch and colors. Replace mottled robe/stone patches with broad wash
groups; simplify trousers to lit/shadow masses and four or five intentional
fold strokes, hair to one dark mass with a few sweeping strands. Thin the
stone side plane, retain two cracks, clean isolated colored alpha fringes.

Portrait environment: use the character only for palette/brush reference;
exclude character/seat. Quiet original Chinese cliff and valley, sparse pine,
thin waterfall, a few bamboo stems, broad low distant mountain washes, winding
pale river, warm ivory mist, open central silhouette area and quiet stone
terrace. No buildings, sun disk, repeated needle mountains, text, UI or grain.
Wide companion: reuse the portrait identity, 3:2 composition, bottom-quarter
terrace and especially quiet right third for live controls.

## Azure Cloud shared reference — initial prompt

Original illustrated game icon, Azure Cloud Meditation technique emblem.
Use attached cultivator only for the restrained ink contour and mineral jade /
blue-grey wash language; do not include character. A single broad blue-grey
cloud curl wraps around a small pale jade rising breath wisp. Three purposeful
tapered brush contours, two broad flat wash value groups, warm ivory highlights;
confident simple asymmetric silhouette readable at 48px. Centered isolated icon
within square canvas, generous consistent empty margin 18% on every side. True
transparent background. No frame, seal, letters, numbers, symbols of a UI state,
outline circle, gold, neon glow, synthetic paper grain, stippling, tiny detail
or shadows outside the painted object. This establishes the visual language of
a matching cultivation game icon set.

## Companion icon prompt packet

Every companion call used that exact Azure Cloud image as the shared reference.
Prompts specified a single original cultivation icon, tapered dark blue-grey
ink contours, mineral jade/blue-grey/ivory wash, two or three broad values,
32–64 px readability, centered square with 18% margins and true transparency.
They prohibited letters, numbers, frames, state marks, gold, neon, extra
decoration, tiny detail and synthetic noise. Subject requests:

- Qi: one upward curling jade breath flame, a loose brush loop around a pale
  core; a taller silhouette distinct from Azure’s horizontal cloud.
- Herbs: medicinal sprig with broad pointed jade leaves, one ivory flower,
  visible stem/root and few vein lines.
- Iron: closed hand with thumb crossing the index/middle fingers, ivory cloth
  wrist wrap, dark jade sleeve, coherent anatomy and no metal armor.
- Forest: three bamboo stems, a few broad leaves and a little path between them.
- Breathing: three widening curved breath strokes over one open cupped palm.
- Meridian: simple headless upper torso, two internal flowing channels and
  three nodes. Subsequent directions below replace the first ornate result.
- Mind: three flat smooth stones stacked horizontally, one upright jade leaf.
- Preparation: shallow grey stone bowl, two herb leaves, small red cloth tie.
- Advancement: three ascending grey stone steps and one upward jade wisp.
- Mortal: closed lotus bud, short stalk and two little leaves.
- Qi Refining: half-open three-petal lotus, broad leaf beneath, no roots.
- Foundation: open five-petal lotus, three broad roots into a low grey base.

## Selected small-icon cleanup prompts

Herbs: preserve plant identity, silhouette, palette, line-and-wash and true
transparency. The 32 px test is too pale and detailed. Make four leaves mostly
medium-dark jade with one pale highlight each, a small ivory flower and two
short simple roots. Remove tiny veins, root filaments and mottled patches.
Keep broad clean color areas, tapered contours and a recognizable medicinal
sprig. Clean colored fringe dots; no new symbols, text, labels or ornaments.

Meridian: the first simplification became pale and glossy and was rejected.
The final edit used that simple torso silhouette as its edit target plus Azure
as brush-style reference: preserve torso and three nodes, replace pale/blurry
rendering with a firm dark tapered contour, medium flat jade body wash, three
clear ivory nodes connected by one dark central channel, and only two curved
shoulder-to-waist lines. No gradients, airbrush, shine, muscle shadows, ripples,
grain, ornament, detached marks, text or frame.
