# Cultivation fiction: reusable progression patterns

Research date: **2026-10-23**. Purpose: inform an original Dao's End design targeting **300 hours of active play**, with reduced offline growth. These are proposals, not verified game features or claims that fiction supplies balanced game rules.

## Scope and provenance

The novel sources are licensed English editions on Wuxiaworld: **A Record of a Mortal's Journey to Immortality** by Wang Yu, **Renegade Immortal** by Er Gen, and **Desolate Era** by I Eat Tomatoes. Their title pages identify Chinese provenance and Yuewen licensing. The first two are labeled xianxia; Desolate Era is labeled xuanhuan. These are Chinese web novels, not interchangeable with Japanese light novels. [RMJI](https://www.wuxiaworld.com/novel/rmji), [Renegade Immortal](https://www.wuxiaworld.com/novel/renegade-immortal), [Desolate Era](https://www.wuxiaworld.com/novel/desolate-era)

The comic reference is **Tales of Demons and Gods**, the cultivation manhua represented by Taxue Comics on Webnovel; its corresponding licensed novel is identified as Chinese on Wuxiaworld. Webnovel's generic English catalogue heading says "Manga"; that is not evidence of Japanese origin. This research does not treat Chinese manhua and Korean manhwa as the same tradition, or claim one universal cultivation hierarchy. [Publisher listing](https://www.webnovel.com/comic/14622895506049901), [licensed novel metadata](https://www.wuxiaworld.com/novel/tales-of-demons-and-gods)

## Six patterns worth adapting

### 1. Preparation competes with immediate cultivation

**Observed:** RMJI chapter 28 explicitly associates medicine-making with slower cultivation, preparation against wounds and poisons, and medicinal assistance overcoming a cultivation bottleneck. [Official chapter 28](https://www.wuxiaworld.com/novel/rmji/rmji-chapter-28)

**Original game proposal:** safe meditation, preparation, and expeditions should compete for a limited activity allocation. A player can invest in better future attempts or pursue immediate production. Preparation must have a visible expected benefit; it should not become a mandatory checklist before every tier. Display predicted outcomes and retain a viable ordinary route without rare ingredients.

### 2. Valuable resources belong to an ecology

**Observed:** the accessible RMJI chapter 834 teaser connects old fruit, a possible guarding beast, consumed fruit, refinement, and potential help with a bottleneck. The resource's danger and use are connected. [Official chapter 834 teaser](https://www.wuxiaworld.com/novel/rmji/rmji-chapter-834/)

**Original game proposal:** location hazards should explain ingredient identity. A mist-fed herb might support stability; a storm-region mineral might support protective equipment. Scouting reveals properties and route risks. Different builds can harvest, fight, or bypass a hazard. Avoid universal "herb number 7" drops and do not gate the campaign behind a tiny random chance.

### 3. A cultivation path changes capabilities

**Observed:** Desolate Era's early training explanation distinguishes energy refinement from body refinement, with different capabilities and training difficulty. It permits developing both rather than describing an exclusive character class. [Official book 1, chapter 6](https://www.wuxiaworld.com/novel/desolate-era/de-book-1-chapter-6)

**Original game proposal:** develop a small number of overlapping approaches. A body method could turn endurance into longer expeditions; a spiritual method could use scanning to locate rare resource opportunities; a sword method could specialize in guardian challenges. Combine techniques through explicit interactions. Do not directly import this novel's methods, names, or a guaranteed superior path. Specialization needs situational strengths, costs, and an affordable way to experiment.

### 4. Advancement changes what existence allows

**Observed:** the same Desolate Era chapter introduces staged thresholds, foundation building, lifespan changes, and tribulations. It treats advancement as a qualitative transformation, with severe fictional danger. [Official book 1, chapter 6](https://www.wuxiaworld.com/novel/desolate-era/de-book-1-chapter-6)

**Original game proposal:** each major realm should add a capability, a recognizable world or character change, and a new kind of challenge. Use scheduled, player-initiated tribulation encounters with preparation previews and retained progress on defeat. Do not copy fictional death rates into a game that asks for hundreds of hours. The chapter supports the pattern, not Dao's End's proposed realm order or numerical rules.

### 5. Understanding can be a separate advancement axis

**Observed:** the Renegade Immortal chapter 908 teaser describes comprehension of a source origin as beneficial to cultivation and a test for inner-disciple status. [Official chapter 908 teaser](https://lite.wuxiaworld.com/novel/renegade-immortal/rge-chapter-908)

**Original game proposal:** represent comprehension through discoveries and demonstrated technique interactions. A short illustrated challenge might ask the player to counter a known hazard using an understood property. Unlock a new technique behavior afterward. Avoid mandatory philosophical quizzes, dialogue mazes, or a second experience bar named Insight that merely grows alongside Qi. The fiction does not establish our challenge design.

### 6. A second beginning gains meaning from changed knowledge

**Observed:** the official Tales of Demons and Gods comic synopsis presents a return to the protagonist's younger self following a lethal ambush, giving him another chance. The licensed novel synopsis explicitly associates the new beginning with knowledge accumulated in his previous life. These establish a second-beginning premise, not a repeatable prestige economy. [Official Taxue Comics listing](https://www.webnovel.com/comic/14622895506049901), [licensed novel synopsis](https://www.wuxiaworld.com/novel/tales-of-demons-and-gods)

**Original game proposal:** reincarnation should preserve discovered relationships, recipe knowledge, selected technique mastery, and tools that accelerate mastered sections. New life conditions can change good strategies. A reset should let players exploit understanding and reach unfamiliar decisions sooner. Define retention rules visibly before confirming. Do not reproduce this story's characters, artifacts, plot, or text.

## What this does and does not justify

### Additional licensed Korean murim comic references

The official WEBTOON synopsis of **Infinite Leveling: Murim** describes a defeated protagonist receiving a quest/level system with increasingly difficult challenges. This supports an adjacent progression pattern: demonstrate growing mastery through escalating objectives rather than one indefinitely increasing meter. It does not establish any particular cultivation realm order or the comic's detailed game rules. [Official WEBTOON series page](https://www.webtoons.com/en/action/infinite-leveling-murim/list?title_no=2676)

The official **Nano Machine** synopsis places martial training within an academy competition and introduces a technological advantage. An original game can adapt bounded training trials and rival benchmarks; its science-fiction mechanism and characters are not part of Dao's End's ink-fantasy direction. [Official WEBTOON series page](https://m.webtoons.com/en/action/nano-machine/list?title_no=4344)

These two observations come from official synopses, not a complete reading of either manhwa. Together with the Chinese web-novel/manhua sources above, they supply distinct reference traditions without treating all Asian progression fiction as interchangeable.

These patterns can connect cultivation, exploration, crafting, techniques, and resets. They do **not** establish 300 hours of enjoyable active play. Fiction delivers surprises through authored events; a game must produce understandable decisions and rewarding feedback. More realms, longer timers, repeated taps, or leaving the app open are not evidence of active content.

Use a finite expansion plan with tested regions, interacting builds, meaningful challenges, and automation of already-solved tasks. Measure the active target separately from elapsed account time. Reduced offline rewards should support the next session without substituting for active exploration; offline absence should not trigger irreversible death, resource loss, or unconsented resets. Exact rates require simulation and playtests, not lore justification.

## Limitations

Research uses title-page metadata and synopses, one early accessible Desolate Era chapter, one accessible RMJI chapter, two official chapter teasers, and a comic synopsis. It is not a complete reading, genre survey, or hands-on game study. Reader reviews, unofficial wikis, and fantranslations were not used as evidence. No detailed later-story mechanic is inferred beyond the accessible text. All adaptations above are original design suggestions requiring approval, implementation, and verification.
