real browser recordings/audio, the native-speed reel, generated production
sources/manifests, rules and test results. Large historical capture collections
and original downloaded audio archive packages are not duplicated into Git.
Their historical reports and exact external source/license records remain.

Current supported evidence entry point: [Living Dao delivery](living-dao/DELIVERY.md).
Historical reports describe their original capture directories; those are not
new claims about this release. Synthetic fixtures are restored with
`python tools/prepare_verification.py`; no real player save or signing key is
part of the public package.
