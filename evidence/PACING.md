# Living Dao pacing evidence

These results use deterministic public actions and earned saves. Simulation
advances quickly in the test runner, but reported seconds are game wall time;
browser walkthroughs separately run at ordinary real speed. None measures
human engagement, enjoyment or active hours.

| Segment | Prior policy | Living Dao | Interpretation |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Fixed Foundation entry to Core Trial | 1,009 s | 1056 s | Combat watching, including optional trials |
| Funding-only retreat after that policy | 1,700 s | 0 s | Removed funding gap, not a claimed increase in fun |
| Fixed Foundation entry to Golden Core | 2,709 s / 45.15 min | 1056 s / 17.60 min | Same no-gear entry boundary |
| Earned Golden Core to Nascent Soul | No content | 517 s / 8.62 min | Six routes, six inheritances, Soul; no preparations |
| Fresh Mortal to Foundation | Not compared here | 1146.1 s / 19.10 min | Public purchases, Azure gathering, optional preparation |
| Fresh through Nascent Soul | Not compared here | 45.32 min | Automated policy, includes waiting; excludes deliberation/input time |

The fresh chain has its own FRESH_JADE_REACHABILITY/FRESH_SOUL_REACHABILITY
reports and earned fixtures. The fixed Foundation entry starts with capped
tutorial practices, 0 Qi and 12 herbs, no chapter gear or history. No new
region rewards are granted for the Golden-to-Soul run. Candidate builds use
only the arts and equipment owned at each boundary. Repeated serialization
must validate; no rare drops, law, preparation or farming is required.

The new policy lowers Core to 24,000 Qi, introduces 2,400 first-clear Qi per
old route and compensates previously cleared routes once on migration.
Sword is two 95% hits; Ward saves focus at healthy HP unless a charge is near.
Ford venom creates a real cleanse niche. Fixed seeded comparisons in
BUILD_COMPARISONS.json isolate builds; they do not prove progression.

The earned new-chapter report lists multiple winning approaches per gate.
Returning refunds only an actual barrier break. Mirror gives a smaller shield
and one indirect retaliation. Thunder answers a charge without stopping basic
pressure. Recovery can preserve endurance HP while offense shortens exposure.
Full numeric rules, priorities and caps are in CONTENT_TABLE.md.

First-route browser play requires eight taps and no scrolling for the measured
route → discovery → requested camp → free equip → continue task. The broader
frontier walkthrough also deliberately selects/cancels a preparation, replaces
an occupied slot and checks spending; its tap count is a different task.

Experimentation, administration and combat watching are not interchangeable.
Human experimentation and enjoyment are unmeasured. The opening still contains
substantial automatic waiting. This candidate does not establish several
satisfying sessions or 300 active hours; no timers were padded to claim them.
Next milestone: observe novice goals, build recognition and dull stretches,
then tune this chapter before specifying realms beyond Nascent Soul.
