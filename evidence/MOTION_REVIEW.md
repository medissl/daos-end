# Runtime motion review

Character: one grounded identity with aligned resting/release cels; sleeve,
hair and small cloth response use fixed outside contours. The painted cape
overlaps a fully painted torso. The seat does not bob. Mortal has plain cloth,
Qi has inward breath, Foundation adds an ivory cape/formation, Golden Core has
a dark teal silhouette/clasp, and Nascent Soul has a white-blue garment and
folded-hand pose with a restrained upper-body echo. Appearance follows attained
realm, independently of spendable Qi. Still progression sheets are in the
gallery; production PNGs and rejected samples are in ART_MANIFEST.json.

Navigation is an interruptible 180 ms content fade; a replacement kills its
predecessor. This is a short transition, not a cinematic full-screen wipe.
Departure gives the guardian a 400 ms arrival; state commits immediately.
Actual cast serials trigger the release cel and distinct sword, flame, ward,
cleanse, returning, thunder and counter effects. Enemy charge/recoil is tied
to combat, not a decorative timer. Breakthrough lasts two seconds and can be
skipped without changing rewards. Hidden effects expire rather than replaying
on return. Reduced motion removes displacement/fades while preserving static
telegraphs, meters, state and earned costume.

Rendered checks cover four sizes, collection sheets and battle states. Real
browser videos cover touch input, rapid destination changes, resize during
transition, discovery, camp, replacement, continuation and earned advancement.
The short reel is cut from those native-speed recordings; cuts omit loading
and repeated waiting, never accelerate simulation. The source recordings and
approximate screenshot timelines remain alongside it. Playback speed is 1×.

This is a modest illustrated cutout/cel presentation. It is not skeletal
full-body animation, lip sync or bespoke animation per each enemy family.
Physical Android smoothness and a human readability/comfort review are pending.
